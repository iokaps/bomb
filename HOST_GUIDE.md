# 💣 The Bomb Game – Host Guide

## Who Is This For?

This is for **event organizers, facilitators, and party planners** who want to host an engaging, fast-paced trivia game. You're the person in charge—the one holding the keys to chaos and fun. If you want to create memorable moments where your audience has a blast answering trivia questions under pressure, you've found the right game.

## When Should You Use It?

Perfect for:
- 🎓 **Classroom icebreakers** – Get students energized and thinking on their feet
- 🤝 **Team building events** – Build camaraderie through competitive fun
- 🎉 **Parties & gatherings** – A hilarious way to keep your guests entertained
- 🏢 **Corporate events & conferences** – Break up the monotony during breaks
- 🎯 **Competitions & tournaments** – Multiple rounds of trivia action

## What You Need

Before you start hosting, gather:
- ✅ **1 Laptop/Desktop** (for you, the host) – This is your command center
- ✅ **1 Big Screen or Projector** (for the presenter display) – So everyone can watch the chaos unfold
- ✅ **WiFi connection** – To connect all the players
- ✅ **2-20 Players** (or more if you're feeling brave!) – Each with a smartphone or tablet
- ✅ **A sense of humor** – Things get competitive fast 😄

## How to Run It (The Host's Playbook)

### Step 1: Start the Game in Host Mode

1. Open the Bomb game on your laptop
2. You'll automatically load in **Host Mode** – this is your mission control
3. You'll see the host control panel with all your game-managing superpowers

### Step 2: Generate Player Links & Get Players Aboard

1. The system generates **player join links** for you
2. Share the link or QR code with your players (however works best—text, email, display on screen)
3. Players scan the QR code or click the link on their devices
4. They'll enter their name and join the **Lobby**
5. Watch them flood in on your host screen—it's satisfying 🚀

### Step 3: Set Up Your Game (The Pre-Game Config)

Before the bombs start ticking, customize your experience:

**Timer Settings:**
- **Fuse Duration** (10-60 seconds) – How long before the bomb explodes?
  - *Shorter* = Higher intensity, faster rounds
  - *Longer* = More thinking time, strategic gameplay
- **Reset on Pass** – Should the timer reset when the bomb passes to another player?
  - *Enabled* = Everyone gets a fresh chance (fairer)
  - *Disabled* = Hot potato style! Timer keeps ticking (more stressful, more fun)

**Question Settings:**
- **Difficulty** (1-5) – How hard should the trivia be?
  - 1 = Easy, crowd-pleasing questions
  - 5 = Brutal brain-teasers
- **Tricky Questions** – Enable if you want sneaky distractor answers that are almost plausible

### Step 4: Prepare the Game

1. Click **Prepare Game**
2. The system generates a pool of questions based on your settings
3. You'll see a progress bar—questions are being created behind the scenes (powered by AI)
4. Once done, you're locked and loaded ✅

### Step 5: The Magic Moment – Start the Game

1. All players are in the lobby? Perfect.
2. Click **Start Game** on your host screen
3. A countdown plays (drama builds!)
4. The game begins and a random player gets the bomb 💣

### Step 6: During Gameplay (Sit Back and Enjoy)

- **On your host screen**: You see game controls, player statuses, and who has the bomb
- **On the big screen** (presenter view): A visually engaging display shows:
  - The ticking bomb timer
  - Current question and bomb holder
  - Players still alive vs. eliminated
  - Leaderboard
- **On player phones**: They see the trivia question and answer options
  - **Correct answer** = Bomb passes to someone else (phew!)
  - **Wrong answer** = New question appears for them
  - **Timer runs out** = BOOM! That player is eliminated 💥

### Step 7: Game Over – Celebrating the Survivor

- The game continues until only **1 player remains**
- That lucky person is crowned the winner! 🏆
- Celebrate them, applaud, enjoy the chaos you just orchestrated

### Pro Tips for the Best Experience

- **Start smaller** – Test with 5-10 players before going bigger
- **Adjust difficulty** – If questions are too hard, players get frustrated; too easy, it's boring
- **Timing is key** – 20-30 second fuse durations are sweet spots for most audiences
- **Hype it up** – Use your host screen to announce eliminations and build tension
- **Have backups** – If WiFi hiccups, the game gracefully handles players rejoining
- **Multiple rounds** – Run 2-3 games in a row to let different players strategize and improve

## Troubleshooting

**Players can't join?**
- Make sure the QR code/link is accessible and WiFi is working
- Share the link directly (copy it from your host screen)

**Game feels slow or questions are boring?**
- Adjust difficulty and tricky questions settings
- Re-prepare with new settings and start a fresh game

**Someone wants to join mid-game?**
- They can! They'll wait in the lobby until the next round
- No need to restart
